Privacy Policy for Myachmyach («МячМяч»)

Effective date: October 29, 2026.

1. Operator and scope

The personal data operator is the developer of the МячМяч app (“we”).

For personal data matters: hello@ballball.ru. App support: hello@ballball.ru.

This Policy explains data processing in the Myachmyach iOS app: finding clubs and partners for padel and other sports, organising matches and training, discovering players, mixed doubles pairs, chats, likes, superlikes, photos and ratings. The Terms of Use are published at https://ball-match.github.io/myachmyach-legal/en/terms/, and this Policy at https://ball-match.github.io/myachmyach-legal/en/privacy/.

2. User age

The app is only for people aged 18 or older. Age is checked at registration using the date of birth provided. You must not provide a false birth date or create an account for a minor. If you discover an account belonging to someone under 18, report it in the app or contact support. We review these reports and take action to block the account and delete data, subject to section 9.

4. Grounds for processing

Account, profile and interaction data are needed to provide the features you request. Device permissions and tracking consent are requested separately where needed. Processing to comply with the law, protect security and defend rights must have an independent lawful basis. Accepting the Terms of Use does not replace separate consent where required.

We process data necessary to perform the Agreement and provide requested features. Optional device permissions and tracking follow your choices in iOS dialogs. Reports and protection of rights require only relevant materials. Where law requires separate consent, accepting this Agreement or Policy does not replace it.

5. Device permissions and your choices

  1. Location: access only while using the app, not continuous background tracking. Refusal limits features that need location. The app receives coordinates at the accuracy you allow in iOS to display the club map. Club requests may include the visible map bounds. We do not publish current device coordinates in your profile or use them for background tracking or player discovery; matching uses your selected city and clubs.
  2. Camera and photos: the camera is used for profile photos; the system photo picker gives access to the images you select, not your entire photo library. Access is limited by your choices and iOS permissions.
  3. Calendar: write-only access is requested to add a selected match or booking event. Other calendar events are not needed for this feature.
  4. Notifications: a push token is used to deliver notifications. Permission to display them can be managed in iOS settings.
  5. Tracking: permission can be managed in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusing does not prevent access to the app's core features.

Camera, location and calendar permissions can also be changed in iOS settings. Withdrawing permission stops the corresponding access going forward but does not itself delete data already transferred; see sections 9–10 for deletion.

6. Analytics, advertising and App Tracking Transparency

We use AppMetrica (Yandex) for analytics and AppsFlyer for installation attribution — identifying the advertising sources through which users discovered the app. Results are used to measure and improve advertising campaigns.

Before activating AppMetrica and starting AppsFlyer, the app requests a decision through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system prompt if no decision has been made and the prompt is available. If a decision already exists or access is restricted by the system, the current permission status applies.

IDFA and tracking across other companies' apps and websites are permitted only with ATT permission. Sending an account identifier to AppMetrica and AppsFlyer to link events also depends on that permission. If permission is refused, the SDKs may operate for analytics and attribution without that tracking; refusal does not disable all analytics. The app disables IDFA collection by these SDKs, IDFV collection in AppsFlyer and transmission of the account identifier to them. IDFV is still used separately for account and device functions. When permission changes, the app applies the new status when next activated.

Sentry is used separately for crash reports and starts independently of ATT; ATT permission is not a switch for error diagnostics.

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertising partners for retargeting or advertising audiences. The event and technical data sent to AppMetrica and AppsFlyer as described above serve analytics and attribution; this is still sharing with third parties. Promo cards are served by our backend; impressions and taps may be recorded with card and account identifiers. Chat contents and photos are not sent to these SDKs as product-event parameters.

By using the service, you agree to processing the data you submit on the stated infrastructure in Germany. Applicable personal data laws, including Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ, continue to apply. Where applicable law requires separate consent or other steps, service use does not replace them.

9. Retention and account deletion

Data is kept only for the period necessary for its purpose and mandatory legal requirements. Specific periods:

Category Period or criterion
Account, profile, photos, sports activity and interactionsWhile needed for an active account; limit for inactive accounts: 3 years since the last sign-in, after which the account is subject to deletion
Messages, reports, blocks and support enquiriesUp to 1 year after account deletion; moderation evidence only as needed for safety and legal claims

Backups and copies held by processorsUp to 30 days after removal from active systems

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account («Удалить аккаунт»). Unlike signing out or removing the app from your device, account deletion ends use of the profile and starts deletion of the associated data.

The profile and account data, photos, likes, superlikes, matches and other interactions are deleted, and chat participation ends. Messages exchanged with other participants may remain in anonymised form, without a link to the deleted profile. Anonymisation removes the link to the sender profile. Message text may itself contain personal information you supplied; removing the profile link does not automatically anonymise that text. To remove such information and attachments, identify the relevant conversation in a request to hello@ballball.ru. Retained conversations are deleted no later than 1 year after account deletion.

Time to complete deletion from active systems: within 30 days of the request; sessions are revoked immediately. Processor copies and backups are cleared within the periods above. If the law requires certain records to be retained, they are not used to continue operating the deleted account. Report and moderation evidence may be retained for up to 1 year after account deletion only for safety and legal claims. If binding law or a court requires retention, we keep only required records for the necessary period, restrict their use to that purpose and provide the basis and period on request unless prohibited by law.

If deletion fails or you need help, contact hello@ballball.ru. Contacting support does not replace the ability to delete the account within the app.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request information about processing and access to your data, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or cessation of unlawful processing and withdraw consent. Where the law provides additional rights, including restriction of processing, objection or data portability, you may also exercise them. You may contact a competent data protection authority or a court.

You can edit available profile fields in the app. For other requests, write to hello@ballball.ru, identifying your account and request. To protect your data, we may ask for the minimum information needed to confirm account ownership; do not send passwords or sign-in codes. Response process and timeframe: we reply by email within 30 days of receipt; any shorter statutory deadline takes precedence.

Withdrawal of consent applies to future processing based on it and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Data required to meet a mandatory legal obligation may be retained independently of withdrawal. If data is necessary for a selected feature, that feature may become unavailable once its processing stops.

11. Moderation and user protection

User photos are reviewed by moderators before publication within 24 hours of upload.

You can report and block a user from a card, profile or chat. We review reports within 24 hours of receipt. We have zero tolerance for prohibited content and behaviour: content that violates the rules is removed and violating accounts are blocked. Detailed prohibitions appear in the Terms of Use. Report content and related material are processed to review reports.

12. Data security

We limit data access to processing purposes and take protective measures appropriate to the data and risks. The app connects to the API over HTTPS and account access is protected by session tokens. Server functions check authorisation; administrative moderation functions are separate from user functions. Report suspected leaks or unauthorised access to hello@ballball.ru for investigation and action.

Do not give other people access to your account. No storage or transmission method provides absolute security; this does not release us from mandatory legal requirements.
